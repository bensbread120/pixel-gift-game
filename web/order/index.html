Your gift game

Fill this in after you order. Nothing is sent from this page: at the end it makes a block of text for you to paste into an Etsy message to me. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can come back to finish.

Package

The one you bought on Etsy.

The player

The person the gift is for. A dog wakes them up and leads them through a village full of your memories. Their character has long hair and a dress.

You

You're waiting at the end of the game with a picnic. Your character has short hair, a jumper and trousers.

The dog

Your dog, a dog you used to have, or the dog you've always wanted. It does most of the talking.

Title and dedication

Shown on the title screen.

Six memories

The dog stops six times on the way, and each stop shows one of these as a picture card with a short title and caption. In order, oldest first works well.

Your message

The heart of the gift. After the picnic, the player reads this page by page. A few short paragraphs work best.

Send it to me

  1. Press Make my order text, then Copy.
  2. On Etsy, open your purchase and choose Message seller (or reply to my message).
  3. Paste the text and send it. For the photos package, attach your six photos to the same message, in memory order.
